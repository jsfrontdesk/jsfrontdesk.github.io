FAQ

Good Questions

Is this a real electrician?

No. Bayswater Breaker & Wire is a fictional business. This is a demo website built by JS Front Desk to show electricians and other local businesses what a website and an AI receptionist could look like. The phone number reaches a live demo of that receptionist.

Are you a real person?

No. The phone line is answered by Alex, a virtual assistant.

Are you licensed and insured?

The owner can confirm license and insurance details when they follow up with you.

Do you give free estimates?

In this demo, estimates are free for larger jobs like panel upgrades, EV chargers and rewiring. For repairs, the starting prices apply to a service call. The owner confirms the final price before any work.

Do you do emergency work?

Call any time. The front desk takes your name, address and problem as a message for the owner, and the owner follows up. If you smell burning or see sparks, hang up and call 911.

How soon can someone come out?

The front desk can't promise a time. It takes your details and the owner follows up to schedule.

What areas do you serve?

The Rockaways (including Far Rockaway), the Five Towns, Long Beach and Queens.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes, homes plus small shops and offices. For bigger commercial jobs the owner follows up.

Can you give me a quote over the phone?

The front desk can give starting prices for common jobs. The owner confirms the final price after understanding the work.

What payment do you accept?

Sample policy: cash, major credit and debit cards, and checks. The owner confirms for each job.

What is your cancellation policy?

Sample policy: please give at least two hours notice if you need to change a scheduled visit. The owner confirms changes.
