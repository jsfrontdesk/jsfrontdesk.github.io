FAQ

Good Questions

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, whenever a chair is open. Appointments get priority, so call or text ahead to skip the wait, especially on Fridays and Saturdays.

How do I book an appointment?

Call or text (347) 440-0360 any time, 24/7. Our front desk will find an open time, book you in, and send a text reminder.

Can I call after hours?

Absolutely. The front desk answers around the clock, so you can book at 2 AM if that's when you remember.

What payment do you accept?

Cash, all major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Tips are appreciated.

What's your cancellation policy?

Life happens. Just call or text at least 2 hours before your appointment and we'll move you to another time.
